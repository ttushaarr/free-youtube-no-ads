FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't YouTube block this like other adblockers? +

Traditional adblockers abort network connections to Google ad servers, which YouTube detects with anti-adblock scripts. BraveTube uses a hybrid fast-forward technique: when an ad is cued, it accelerates the player to 16x speed and jumps directly to duration in <50ms. YouTube's servers register that the ad played legitimately.

Does the Android app require rooting or Google Play Services? +

No root required, and no Google Play Services dependency. It runs on any Android phone (Android 8.0 Oreo through Android 15+). You can also sign into your Google account inside the app if you want your subscriptions and watch history saved.

How does background playback work on Android? +

YouTube normally pauses video whenever the page is hidden or the phone screen turns off. BraveTube neutralizes the HTML5 Page Visibility API, telling YouTube that the player is always visible and focused, enabling seamless background playback.

Is BraveTube free and safe? +

Yes, 100% free and open-source. There are zero trackers, analytics scripts, or remote server dependencies.
