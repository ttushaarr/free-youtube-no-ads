## 📺 1. `BraveTube-TV.apk` (For Android TV & Smart TVs)

Specifically compiled and optimized for **Android TV, Google TV, and Smart TVs**.

### 🌟 TV-Optimized Highlights:
- **🎮 Full TV Remote D-Pad Navigation:** Features built-in spatial navigation for effortless couch browsing using your TV remote arrow keys (Up, Down, Left, Right, OK).
- **🕹️ Media Key Mapping:** TV remote buttons for Play/Pause, Fast-Forward (10s), and Rewind (10s) are mapped directly to video controls.
- **🖼️ Android TV Leanback Banner:** Includes the mandatory `tv_banner.png` so the app appears on your Android TV home screen row.
- **🚫 Touchscreen Requirement Disabled:** Declares `touchscreen=false` in the manifest so TV app installers accept it without compatibility warnings.
- **💡 Continuous Display WakeLock:** Keeps the TV display active during playback so your TV's ambient screensaver or sleep timer never interrupts your video.
- **⚡ Ad-Free 4K HDR:** Turbo-skips in-stream ads in <50ms and suppresses anti-adblock modals.

### 📺 Compatible Smart TV Devices:
- **Philips Smart 4K TV / Ambilight TV**
- **Sony Bravia (Google TV & Android TV)**
- **Chromecast with Google TV (HD & 4K)**
- **Xiaomi Mi TV / Mi Box / TV Stick**
- **TCL, Hisense, OnePlus, Skyworth Android TVs**
- **Nvidia Shield TV & Shield TV Pro**
- **Amazon Fire TV & Fire TV Stick**

## 📱 2. `BraveTube.apk` (For Android Phones & Tablets)

Optimized for **mobile devices with touchscreens**.

### 🌟 Phone-Optimized Highlights:
- **🎧 Screen-Off Background Audio:** Locks your screen or switches apps while audio keeps playing (no YouTube Premium needed).
- **🖼️ Floating Picture-in-Picture (PiP):** Tap Home or swipe to home to shrink the video into a floating mini-player.
- **🚫 Complete Ad-Block:** Intercepts ad domains at the network level and fast-forwards video ads in <50ms.
- **👆 Touch-Friendly Mobile UI:** Full gestures, pinch-to-zoom, and standard mobile YouTube controls.
